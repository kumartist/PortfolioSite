Computing & Computing History

I enjoy reading books and watching videos about video game console architecture, video game development, computer graphics, and computing history.

During college, I had lunch with Bjarne Stroustrup (creator of C++) and Walter Bight (creator of Dlang). I also ran into Mark Cerny (PlayStation 5 architect and game development legend) at the Game Developer Conference. Contrary to popular advice, I often enjoy meeting my heroes.

Some cool books, videos, and blogs I enjoyed:

  • Mike Shah on YouTube. Mike was my game engines & computer graphics professor during college, and I was a teaching assistant for his courses. He organized the aformentioned lunches with Bjarne Stroustrup and Walter Bight.
  • Modern Vintage Gamer on YouTube
  • Game Engine Architecture by Jason Gregory
  • Andy Gavin's blog about the development of Crash Bandicoot (1996)
  • Mark Cerny's presentation on PlayStation 5 architecture
